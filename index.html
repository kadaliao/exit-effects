Made one? Submit it in one click.

Press Submit on your effect in Exit. It checks the effect, sends it from your GitHub account and shows you the bot’s answer. Your coding agent can do it for you, too.

  1. Press SubmitOn your effect in Exit 0.4 or later. Sign in with GitHub once, or ask your coding agent.
  2. Checked on a MacA bot compiles it, checks the first and last frames and the speed, and renders a preview.
  3. Here for everyoneOnce the checks pass it shows up on this page and in every Exit’s Market within minutes.
